FAQ

Questions before you choose a plan

Can I switch between Lite, Pro and Prime later?+

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time, just contact FleetHive or start a new plan selection and our team will help you transition smoothly.

What happens after my Tag Plan's free 3 months?+

Your tracking subscription renews at ₦3,000/month or ₦30,000/year (saving ₦6,000). The renewal price is never charged during your first purchase, and your tracking will pause if you choose not to renew.

Do I need to buy hardware add-ons separately?+

No. Hardware and software add-ons are optional and can be selected right inside your checkout form, the cost is added to your total automatically.

Can I add more than one plan to a single order?+

Yes. Use "Add Another Plan" during checkout if you're managing different vehicle categories, or want to combine a subscription plan with a Tag Plan.

How does installation work?+

Choose whether an installer visits your location or you'd rather visit an installation centre, then pick a preferred date and time. Our team will confirm and follow up within 24 hours.

What payment methods are accepted?+

You can pay securely with Paystack (card, bank or transfer) or send a direct Bank Transfer. Bank Transfer orders are marked pending until our team verifies payment.
