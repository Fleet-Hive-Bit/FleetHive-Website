From the horse's mouth

Why I started FleetHive.

I did not start FleetHive to build another tracking app. I started it because I kept seeing the same problem, over and over, with vehicle owners and businesses across Nigeria: not knowing where a vehicle is, or what it is doing, until something has already gone wrong.

That uncertainty costs people money, time and peace of mind. A parent worried about a car their child is driving. A business owner who cannot account for a vehicle on the road. A logistics company that finds out about a problem hours too late. I have seen how much simpler life gets once that uncertainty is removed.

FleetHive exists to give vehicle owners and businesses across Africa better visibility, better control and better access to smart mobility technology.

We are starting from Nigeria, building for Africa, with the ambition to eventually serve a global mobility market. Not because it sounds impressive, but because the same problem exists everywhere people own and move vehicles.

That is why FleetHive exists.

Victor Shinegba Founder & CEO, FleetHive
