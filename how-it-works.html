How FleetHive works

Know More, Control More

FleetHive helps you keep track of your vehicles, understand what they are doing and make better decisions without the guesswork.

What FleetHive gives you

Six simple ways to know more and control more

Every FleetHive solution answers the same question in a different way, what is happening with my vehicle right now, and what should I do about it.

01 · Vehicle Tracking

Know where your vehicle is, right now.

In simple terms

FleetHive shows you where your vehicle is while it is moving, on a simple map you can check anytime.

Real life example

Instead of calling your driver to ask where he is, you open FleetHive and see the vehicle's current location and movement straight away.

Why it matters

You get current location, movement, trip history and visibility across every vehicle you manage, so you always know what is going on.

Stop guessing where your vehicle is. Know.

Start Tracking →
02 · Trips & Activity

See where your vehicle has been.

In simple terms

FleetHive keeps a record of every trip, so you can understand how a vehicle has actually been used, not just where it is right now.

Real life example

You can look back at a trip and see where the vehicle went and when it moved, from start to finish.

Why it matters

Trip playback, journey history and activity records give you a clear picture of vehicle usage over time.

Get Started
03 · Smart Alerts

Never miss something important.

In simple terms

FleetHive sends you an alert the moment something worth knowing happens with your vehicle.

Real life example

If a vehicle moves unexpectedly or starts overspeeding, you get notified straight away instead of finding out later.

Why it matters

Unexpected movement, overspeeding and other important activity are flagged automatically, so you can act quickly.

Know when something needs your attention.

Choose a Plan →
04 · Smart Zones

Know when a place that matters is entered or left.

In simple terms

FleetHive lets you mark important areas, such as your office, depot, home or a customer location, and get notified when a vehicle enters or leaves them.

Real life example

You set up a zone around your depot and get an alert the moment a vehicle leaves it, without watching the map yourself.

Why it matters

Smart Zones give you better visibility over vehicle movement around the places that matter most to you.

Start Tracking
05 · Vehicle Intelligence

Turn vehicle activity into information you can actually use.

In simple terms

FleetHive does not just show where a vehicle is. It helps you understand vehicle activity and turn that into decisions you can act on.

Real life example

Instead of scrolling through raw trip data, you get simple summaries and reports that show how your vehicles are actually being used.

Why it matters

Depending on your plan and hardware, this can include usage patterns, driver activity, fleet reports and operational insights, so you can make better decisions with confidence.

View Pricing →

Beyond vehicles, assets, parcels and orders, tracked with one small reusable tag.

06 · Fleet Tags

Keep an eye on what's moving, not just who's driving.

In simple terms

A Fleet Tag is a small, reusable device you attach to whatever needs tracking, whether that is a vehicle, an asset, a parcel or an order.

Real life example

A business attaches a Fleet Tag to an order in transit and can see its status and location until it is delivered.

Why it matters

Fleet Tags give you an additional, flexible way to track what matters, alongside the Lite, Pro and Prime vehicle tracking plans, not instead of them.

How it works

Install, Connect, Monitor, Act

From the moment your device is installed to the moment you act on what FleetHive shows you, here is exactly what happens.

01

Install

A FleetHive technician installs the tracking device in your vehicle. You do not need any technical knowledge, we handle the setup for you.

02

Connect

Once the device is installed, your vehicle is connected to the FleetHive platform and linked to your account, ready to use.

03

Monitor

Log in anytime to see your vehicle's location, trips, activity and alerts, all in one simple dashboard.

04

Act

Use what FleetHive shows you to respond to unusual activity, understand trips, protect your vehicle and make informed decisions.
